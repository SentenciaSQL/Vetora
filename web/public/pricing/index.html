About LunaVeta

LunaVeta is a cloud-based SaaS platform for veterinary clinics. It helps clinics manage pets, owners, medical records, vaccinations, appointments, communications, and day-to-day operations through web and mobile applications.

FAQ

What is LunaVeta?

LunaVeta is software for veterinary clinics. Staff use it to keep pet records, schedule appointments, record vaccinations, and run the clinic. Pet owners can use the mobile app to follow their own pets.

Can I use LunaVeta on mobile?

Yes. There is a mobile app and a web app. Buying, changing, or canceling a subscription is available in the web app, not in the mobile app.

Can I manage multiple pets and owners?

Yes. A clinic can register many pets and their owners. Pet owners do not count toward the staff account limit of the plan.

Is LunaVeta available in Spanish and English?

Yes. The web app and the mobile app can be used in Spanish or English.

How does billing work?

A clinic administrator creates an account and chooses a plan in the web app. Payments are processed by Paddle in USD. The usual cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Refund requests are described in the refund policy.
